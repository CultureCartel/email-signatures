# What the best email signatures do (read 2026-10-08)

Sources are mostly signature-tool vendors, so treat their numbers as rules of thumb.

## Patterns that recur in the examples people cite

| Layout in build.mjs | Modelled on | The idea |
|---|---|---|
| mono | Four Design, Bybrand | One colour (black). Logo, a hairline, details. |
| oneline | Presentation Partners | Only the essentials, spaced by separators. |
| biglogo | Relish Kitchen (a restaurant), We Love to Travel | A large logo shapes the signature; one social icon beside it. |
| booking | Hotel signatures (Rocketseed example) | A call to action that matches the business: book a table, private events. |
| banner | Hotel banner signatures | A photo strip under the details, linked to the site. |
| signoff | Four Seasons leadership signature | A minimal, serif sign-off. |
| colourbar | Nine-Eighteen | Small serif or sans text, icons under a horizontal rule, a colour bar. |
| icons | The Home Edit | Minimal line icons in front of each detail. |
| directions | Restaurant guidance | Address, a map link, how to find you. |
| centred | Luxury hotel signatures | Logo on top, one centre axis. |

## Rules most sources agree on

- Name, role and contact first; the logo below or beside, never a big image on top (preview panes show the top).
- Readable in under three seconds. Left-aligned single column scans best on a phone.
- About 600px wide at most. Body text 12 to 16px. Logo images small and under 100 KB.
- Inline styles and tables only. Test in Outlook, Gmail and Apple Mail.
- Hospitality: link Instagram, give a booking or directions link.
